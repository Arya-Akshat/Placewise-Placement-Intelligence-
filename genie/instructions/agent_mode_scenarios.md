# Agent Mode Scenarios

## 1. Why Department Placement Declined
**Decomposition**:
- Compare current year placement rate to previous year for the specified department.
- Identify top companies that hired from the department last year but not this year.
- Analyze skill requirements of those companies vs. current student skills.

## 2. Skill Gaps Identification
**Decomposition**:
- Identify the most requested skills by top paying companies this year.
- Aggregate current student skills in the target department.
- Output the difference as skill gaps.

## 3. Targeting Companies
**Decomposition**:
- Find companies that hire for the roles students are most interested in.
- Filter by average package > 5 LPA.
- Recommend target companies for outreach.

## 4. Candidate Ranking Explanations
**Decomposition**:
- Fetch candidates matching the skill criteria.
- Rank by GPA and past project scores.
- Generate a summary explanation for why the top 3 candidates are the best fit.
